Sutra is a specialized mobile CRM built for entrepreneurs in network marketing and direct sales. It works as a smart address book and personal assistant that handles client database routine and automates the field’s main tool—structured post-sale follow-up.

Who it is for
 * Network marketing managers and brand partners who want to move from chaotic notebooks and notes to a simple, repeatable system.
 * Direct sales professionals selling physical products (functional nutrition, supplements, cosmetics) where cumulative effect and feedback matter.
 * Mentors who need an easy way to teach newcomers how to work with clients without heavy corporate CRM software.

What it is for
The main goal is not to lose clients and to help them reach results.
It solves a common problem: as the base grows, the manager forgets who to contact and when—to ask about first impressions or remind them that the product is running out.

The app lets you:
 * Keep the full interaction history in one card (contact, initial need/request, product purchased).
 * Tune the system to your assortment (customizable pick lists).
 * Guide each client through a proven touchpoint schedule to collect feedback on time and make follow-up sales.

Key advantages and features
 * Built-in 0-3-7-15 algorithm
   This is the strongest advantage. The system tells you when to reach out using a classic care pattern:
   * Day 0: Order placement, usage instructions.
   * Day 3: Check-in (received the order, tried it, liked taste/format).
   * Day 7: Regularity check (still using it, any first sensations).
   * Day 15: First results, related product or business opportunity, planning the next order.
 * Full customization
   You fill directories yourself (client requests and products). The app is not tied to one company—you enter current line names and common client needs (e.g. weight management, dry skin, energy recovery).
 * Focus on customer LTV
   With push notifications, no buyer is left behind after the first purchase. Clients feel cared for, get results from the product, and are more likely to become loyal repeat buyers.
 * Minimalism and speed
   Unlike bulky CRM for large businesses, there is no extra clutter—only what a field partner needs to log data in a few taps right after a meeting or chat.

Pro subscription
The free tier of Sutra already covers the core workflow—clients and 0-3-7-15 touchpoints. Pro is for those who are actively growing their base and want to work without caps or distractions.

What Pro includes:
 * No ads — no banners, more screen space, fewer distractions while working with clients.
 * No limits — unlimited client cards and entries in the Products and Client requests directories (free tier: up to 10 clients and up to 10 items per directory).
 * Cloud sync — backup and moving data between devices via the cloud (included with Pro; on the free tier, data stays on the phone only).
 * Same notifications and touchpoint logic — morning daily plan, 0-3-7-15 schedule, and full history on the client card work the same as on the free tier.
